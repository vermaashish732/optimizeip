§ III · Manifesto

We treat patents the way a great editor treats a manuscript.

Movement · I

An idea is only as valuable as the claim that protects it.

We write claims that survive prosecution and litigation. Not templates — bespoke drafts, engineered around the invention and the market it enters.

Movement · II

Every portfolio hides a licensing case waiting to be built.

Through portfolio mining, EoU mapping and reverse engineering, we surface the assets that are already infringed — and translate them into monetizable positions.

Movement · III

Read the record before you ship the product.

Prior-art searches, FTO opinions and stress tests. We tell you what is out there — plainly, without softening the verdict — so you deploy capital with clarity.

Movement · IV

File once. File everywhere. File to grant.

Global drafting and prosecution across the USPTO, EPO, JPO, CNIPA and the Indian Patent Office. One studio. One editor. One coherent portfolio.
